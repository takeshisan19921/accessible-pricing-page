Questions

What happens when I go over my label limit?

Labels keep printing. We email you at 80% and 100% of the limit, and extra labels cost € 0,05 each until the next month starts.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?

Yes. Switching to yearly charges the rest of the year at the yearly price, minus what you already paid this month.

Do prices include VAT?

No. Prices are shown without 21% Dutch VAT, which is added on the invoice. Businesses elsewhere in the EU with a VAT number are billed with reverse charge.

Can I cancel at any time?

Monthly plans stop at the end of the current month. Yearly plans run to the end of the paid year and are not renewed.
